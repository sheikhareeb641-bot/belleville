Happy Teeth Club

Six easy ways to keep your smile shine & healthy

Read these with a parent, then try the games below to practice what you learned!

1. Brush Twice a Day

Brush in the morning and before bed for two whole minutes — sing a happy song so you brush long enough!

Use a pea-sized dot of toothpaste.
2. Eat Tooth-Friendly Food

Apples, carrots, cheese and milk make strong teeth. Too many sweets and fizzy drinks cause cavities.

Drink water after snacks to rinse your teeth.
3. Visit the Dentist

The dentist is a tooth helper, not scary! Regular check-ups keep small problems from becoming big ones.

Most kids visit every six months.
4. Floss Between Teeth

Your toothbrush cannot reach the tiny gaps between teeth — floss gently once a day to move plaque away.

Ask a grown-up to help at first.
5. Protect Your Teeth

Wear a mouthguard when playing sports, and never use your teeth to open packets or bottles!

Strong teeth are for smiling and chewing.
6. Baby Teeth Matter

Baby teeth help you speak and smile, and they save space for adult teeth. Keep them healthy until they fall out on their own.

Wiggle loose teeth — never force them.

This page is for fun, general awareness only. For advice about your own teeth, always ask a dentist or a parent to book a check-up.

For Parents

Helping small smiles grow strong

  • Supervise brushing until about age 8 so no plaque is missed.
  • Make it routine — same time, same order, every day.
  • Keep sugary snacks to mealtimes instead of all day.
  • Book the first dental visit by the first birthday.
  • Celebrate brave visits — positive words beat scary words.
