About Oral Health

Looking after your teeth, gums & mouth

Dental or oral health concerns the teeth, the gums and the whole mouth. When we take good care of our mouth, we protect more than a smile — we protect chewing, speech, confidence and overall well-being.

Preventive care is the foundation: brushing twice a day, flossing daily and visiting the dentist regularly helps maintain oral health and stops small problems from becoming painful, expensive treatments.

  • Brush two minutes, twice a day with fluoride toothpaste
  • Floss once a day to clean between the teeth
  • Visit your dentist for a check-up every six months
  • Limit sugary snacks and drinks between meals
